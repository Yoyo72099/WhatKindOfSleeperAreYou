why I made this

For a long time, I couldn’t fall asleep. I’d lie in bed with my brain running at full speed, counting the hours until my alarm. I tried everything: counting sheep, white noise, scrolling until my eyes gave up.

what this is

Along the way, I noticed that almost everyone I asked had their own strange little trick for falling asleep. Someone can only sleep with a stuffed animal. Someone pretends to sleep until it accidentally becomes real. How Do You Fall Asleep? is a collection of those tricks.

( If you’re reading this at 3 AM: you’re not the only one awake. )

Made by Yoyo Fu
Core 3 Design Studio, Parsons, Fall 2026
Thanks to everyone who shared their bedtime secrets with me.

This project is for fun, not medical advice. If you’ve had trouble sleeping for a long time, please talk to a doctor.
